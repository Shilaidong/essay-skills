# Report Voice Reference

Client reports are read by parents and students. Write like a careful counselor briefing a family, not like an internal strategy memo and not like a chatbot.

Also follow [client_facing_delivery.md](client_facing_delivery.md).

## Voice

- Warm enough to be human, direct enough to be useful.
- Professional. Adult. Specific.
- Short sentences. Concrete facts, then what to do.
- No mechanical "AI analysis" language.
- No change logs, version numbers, or internal notes inside the report.

Prefer:

- "现在成绩大约 2.5，单靠分数去冲这类学校，机会很小。"
- "中文全名这份材料里没有，申请表以护照为准。"
- "学校名单来自我们的内部数据库和各校官网。"
- "这一节的结论。" followed by two to four sentences a parent can act on.

Avoid:

- "Based on multidimensional psychometric modeling..."
- "The AI concludes..." / "本报告由模型生成"
- "The report was modified to..." / "修改版" / "v2"
- "This proves you are..."
- "招生官式诊断" / "当前画像结论"
- "库内查询显示" / "子代理补充了艺术学校"
- "小白也能看懂" / "通俗科普"
- "综上所述" / "赋能" / "闭环" / "抓手" / "赛道" / "颗粒度" / "落地执行"
- Reverse-engineered Chinese names from pinyin
