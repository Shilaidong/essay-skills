# Privacy and Fact Policy

- Do not use one student's private details in another student's report.
- Keep reusable templates anonymous.
- Do not invent awards, rankings, scores, official paths, or names.
- Distinguish `事实`, `学生/家长陈述`, `顾问判断`, and `推断`.
- Names: only transcript, passport, official ID, or explicit confirmation. Never guess Chinese characters from pinyin. If uncertain, omit the name from client files.
- When a fact is uncertain, internal notes may use `需核验`. Client text: `还需要核对`.
- When a required fact is missing, internal notes may use `信息待补齐`. Client text: `还需要补充`.
- Never upgrade an award level unless there is a certificate or official result.
- For sensitive family budget/aid information, avoid unnecessary detail in general summaries.
- Divorce, illness, and similar family facts belong in the essay discussion only if the student wants them there. Do not put them in the profile table as drama.
